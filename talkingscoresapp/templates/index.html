Sheet music, in words you can read bar by bar.

Open a MusicXML file and Talking Scores writes out every bar: the rhythm, the pitch, the dynamics. Read it with a screen reader, a braille display, a magnifier, or your eyes, and hear any bar on its own.

How to get a MusicXML file from MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale or Dorico

Every notation program writes MusicXML, usually under Export rather than Save.

  • MuseScore - File, Export, then MusicXML.
  • Sibelius - File, Export, MusicXML.
  • Finale - File, Export, MusicXML, Uncompressed.
  • Dorico - File, Export, MusicXML.

A PDF or a scan cannot be read here. The music has to have been typed into a notation program.

What Talking Scores does and does not do

It writes out the notes, rests, rhythms, dynamics and repeats a MusicXML file holds, bar by bar, in the words you choose. Every bar range can be played back, and the whole reading can be downloaded as a page, as plain text or as uncontracted braille.

It does not read PDFs, images or scans, and it does not teach the music. Lyrics, chord symbols and figured bass are read only where the file records them.

What is kept and for how long

The file you open and the reading written from it stay on this server so your link keeps working. Nothing is listed publicly and nothing is shared.
