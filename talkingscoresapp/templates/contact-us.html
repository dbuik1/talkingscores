Reporting a score that does not read correctly

Three things make a report quick to act on:

  • The MusicXML file itself, attached.
  • The bar number, and what the reading says about it.
  • What the bar actually contains.

The notation program that wrote the file is worth naming too, since each one writes MusicXML slightly differently.

What happens to an email

An email address sent here is used to reply and for nothing else. It is not passed to anyone, added to a list, or used to get in touch about anything other than the message it came with.

An enquiry may be discussed with other people or published as an example, with nothing identifying who sent it.
