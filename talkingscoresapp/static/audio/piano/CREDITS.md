# Piano recordings

These recordings are from the FluidR3 GM soundfont by Frank Wen, taken from the
acoustic grand piano set in
[gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), and
used under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

One recording is kept every three semitones from A0 to C8. A note between two of
them is played from the nearer recording at a faster or slower rate. The
attribution is shown to readers in the playback settings panel of a score.
